A Tier-0 Behavioral Interlock for Agentic AI: a deterministic, on-device single-token self-twin that gates a graduated governance action — with a pre-registered map of what it catches, what it cannot, and why the blind spot is representation-independent Randolph James Ferlic, M.D. and Kimberly Kate Ferlic — Fieldstone Analytics, LLC, Austin, TX, USA Preprint · Zenodo DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23504157 · CC-BY 4.0 · Community: spiral-domain-encoder-campaign · The agent-behavioral-interlock, swarm, robust-aggregation, and compression-enabled-governance embodiments described herein are the subject of a companion United States provisional patent application, filed before this deposit under a file-first policy — see the notice below. Abstract As autonomous and software agents are given delegated authority to call tools, touch data, and act, operators need a runtime check that an agent is still behaving as commissioned — and a graduated way to respond when it is not. Emerging enterprise frameworks score agent risk from identity, endpoint, and network signals in a cloud control plane. This record characterizes a complementary primitive those signals do not provide: a deterministic, label-free, on-device behavioral interlock. A frozen single-token encoder — raw window → fixed projection → nearest-of-≤256-cell codebook → one-byte token, nearest-centroid distance as a novelty score — is pointed at an agent's own activity trace (tool calls, timing, data volumes, egress, and order-aware sequence features), commissioned into a per-agent behavioral self-twin, and used to gate a graduated control action (allow, require approval, restrict scope, halt). Across a pre-registered battery — a 12-sim core study over four agent archetypes, a GPU-trained deep-representation baseline, 13-sim single-agent and 10-sim swarm Tier-3/4/5 adversarial batteries, agentic-swarm and compression-governance studies, and a cross-architecture on-silicon reduction to practice — the primitive is mapped honestly: predictions fixed before the measurements, negatives kept. It detects configuration/model change, task and scope drift, overt exfiltration, and reordering attacks (AUROC ≈ 0.99–1.00), at a calibrated false-alarm rate, in a 3.7 kB model that runs deterministically (0/256 token flips, bit-exact integer port) in 60–146 µs on two commodity microcontrollers. It is blind to on-manifold misuse (AUROC ≈ 0.50), and this blindness is representation-independent: no self-supervised, autoencoder, or discriminatively-trained deep representation detects it either, and the discriminative one collapses even on the departures the token catches (0.665 vs 0.990). Per-agent commissioning is mandatory (thresholds do not transfer across agents); commissioning-data poisoning is not removed by robust statistics (trusted commissioning is required); individually-sub-threshold collusion needs multi-party aggregation, which scales with swarm size and whose interaction-graph form catches communication-topology shifts, while emergent compositional synergy is representation-independently blind and needs a semantic monitor; and a modest review budget cuts simulated harm by two-thirds. Compressing behavior to one deterministic byte also yields governance a raw-event monitor cannot have — ~1-byte operation over bandwidth-metered or one-way channels, collector-free decentralized swarm aggregation, and content-confidentiality scoped honestly as *not* anonymity (the byte re-identifies the agent). The honest conclusion is a defense-in-depth layer with a measured deployment contract, not a complete defense — and a theorem-like boundary (on-manifold blindness is fundamental) that makes an independent semantic monitor and multi-party aggregation structurally necessary rather than optional. Highlights · A deterministic, on-device Tier-0 interlock for agents. A frozen one-byte self-twin pointed at an agent's activity trace detects configuration/model change, task and scope drift, overt exfiltration, and reordering attacks at AUROC ≈ 0.99–1.00, at a calibrated ≈5% false-alarm rate, in a 3.7 kB model — reduced to practice on a Cortex-M4F and a Cortex-M33 with 0/256 token flips, a bit-exact integer port, and 60–146 µs per decision (33–78× inside a 5 ms budget), on hardware a cloud control plane cannot reach. · The central result is a representation-independent negative. On-manifold misuse — harm carried by actions drawn from the agent's normal distribution, where the malice is semantic — is at chance (≈0.50) not only for the token but for every classical detector and every deep learned representation tried (self-supervised Transformer, generative autoencoder, discriminatively-trained GRU). The blindness is a property of the threat, not a deficiency of a feature space — which reframes defense-in-depth from best practice to structural necessity. · Watch the input, not a learned embedding. No GPU-trained deep representation of behavior beats the input-informative token on the hard case (subtle drift: token 0.990 vs the best deep model 0.785), and the discriminatively-trained one collapses out-of-distribution (0.665) — a design rule with teeth, and an economic one (no GPU monitor needed). · The self-twin is per-agent and label-free. Thresholds do not transfer across agents even of the same archetype (own-agent 0.05 vs sibling 0.89 false-alarm); unsupervised, clean-labeled, and 30%-label-noise commissioning are indistinguishable; agents onboard in tens of windows. · Collusion needs aggregation; swarms scale. Individually-sub-threshold coordination (each agent flagged ≈27%) is decisively caught by a multi-party aggregate (AUROC 0.975, flagged 85%); detection scales with swarm size (0.83 at N=2 → 1.00 by N=32); and an interaction-graph self-twin catches a communication-topology shift invisible to activity monitoring (0.999 vs 0.498). The swarm battery converts correlation, churn, and evasion into eight concrete robustness requirements (de-correlate common mode, swarm-level calibrate, standardize, size-normalized energy-not-count, temporal-aggregate, cover enough agents, attest membership). · The second boundary: emergent synergy. A harmful composition of individually-in-envelope actions is at chance for every behavioral and interaction-graph aggregate — the swarm-level analogue of the on-manifold blind spot — and is disclosed, not claimed, as the composed semantic/orchestration monitor's regime. · Trusted commissioning is a requirement, not a nicety. Commissioning-data poisoning is a real vulnerability (detection 1.00 → 0.62 at a 20% poisoned fraction) that a robust, trimmed fit does not remove — poison that defined "normal" is not a high-residual outlier — so attested commissioning is a security property on-device statistics cannot supply. · The byte changes what governance is possible. Reducing a window to one deterministic, non-invertible symbol makes governance bandwidth-minimal (≈1 byte/decision over metered or one-way/data-diode channels), collector-free decentralizable (gossip aggregate identical to centralized; coordination detected from shared bytes alone at ≈0.91), and content-confidential — honestly scoped as not anonymity (feature-reconstruction R² ≈ 0.62; the byte is a stable pseudonym that re-identifies the agent at ≈0.98) — properties a raw-event or cloud-resident monitor cannot have. · Closed-loop value, quantified. A graduated response cuts net harm by two-thirds at a review budget ≈ the base rate of harmful actions; over-gating adds disruption without preventing more harm, so the optimal budget is near the base rate, not maximized. · Honest negatives make the positives trustworthy. That no deeper model beats the byte forecloses "a bigger model will catch it," that the energy aggregate resists spread-to-evade tells you which aggregate to deploy, that a slow ramp is caught by a cumulative-sum statistic forecloses slow-burn evasion, and that robust statistics do not fix poisoning makes trusted commissioning mandatory — leaving on-manifold as the only true stealth. · One primitive, a new entity type. The program's prior laws — per-entity self-twin, watch-the-input/feature-collapse, geometry-gated drift compensation, and privacy-preserving multi-party aggregation — each transfer to the agent entity with no new algorithm, now gating a graduated governance action. The transferable lesson The through-line is the program's founding premise, now tested in agent governance: the value is cost, determinism, and footprint — not accuracy. Distinguishing a drifting or exfiltrating agent from a commissioned one is not hard in an absolute sense — a one-class SVM (0.994), a local-outlier-factor method (0.993), and a Mahalanobis detector over the whole swarm state (0.90 vs the aggregate's 0.99) all do comparably, and all are equally blind on-manifold. The result is that the cheapest possible monitor — one frozen, deterministic, auditable byte, with no gradient and no cloud round-trip — matches them on accuracy and runs where they cannot: on the agent's own microcontroller, bit-exact across two architectures at 60–146 µs in 3.7 kB, and over a one-way or metered channel at one byte per decision. An in-house team can reproduce the AUROC in a week; it cannot reproduce the deployable Tier-0 package. And the map is drawn with its two honest, representation-independent blind spots — on-manifold single-agent misuse and emergent compositional synergy of a swarm — so that an independent semantic or orchestration monitor and multi-party aggregation are structurally necessary layers, and the interlock states precisely where it stops and hands off cleanly. Data and reproducibility All agent activity traces are synthetic and generated at run time by the runners — order-structured action sequences with timing, data-volume, egress, write, and scope attributes across four agent archetypes and swarms of up to thirty-two agents; no external corpus is required. The reproducibility archive (PAPER_65_ZENODO_ARCHIVE.zip) contains the pre-registered simulation runners (the ABST probe and 12-sim core battery, the GPU deep-representation baseline, the 13-sim single-agent and 10-sim swarm Tier-3/4/5 adversarial batteries, the agentic-swarm battery, and the compression-as-governance demonstrations), a structured result record (JSON), the nine figures and their builder, and a README. The on-silicon measurements were taken on commodity Arm Cortex-M4F and Cortex-M33 microcontrollers; the device firmware is not redistributed (the fixed decision stage is exported by the golden exporter). Every study fixes its seed; the encoder is deterministic and its integer port is bit-exact. Absolute paths, cloud handles, and personal identifiers are scrubbed, and no company or partner names appear in the archive. This deposit extends the program's filed and published frozen-token methods to a new entity type — an autonomous or software agent — and introduces the new agent-interlock, swarm multi-party, robust-aggregation, and compression-enabled-governance embodiments that are the subject of the companion provisional described above.
