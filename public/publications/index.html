Research Publications

Randolph J. Ferlic, M.D.  &  Kimberly K. Ferlic

Austin, Texas, USA

Fieldstone Analytics develops a frozen, deterministic single-token encoder — a sub-milliwatt “decision token” that quantizes a window of sensor signal to one auditable byte for edge and ambient intelligence. The open-access preprints and datasets below characterize its capabilities, limits, and deployment behavior across sensing modalities.
